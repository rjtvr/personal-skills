# Check-ins

Check-ins keep the roadmap honest.

## Order

1. **Review before planning.** Go through last session's this-week actions and 90-day
   milestones. For each: done, partly done, not started, or dropped.
2. **Ask what happened** for anything not done — without judgement. "What got in the way?"
3. **Look for patterns.** The same kind of milestone dropped repeatedly is information: the
   goal may not be wanted, the size may be wrong, or the approach may not suit how they work.
4. **Adjust** — then focus the rest of the session on 1–2 areas.

## Handling missed milestones

Missed milestones aren't failures to be scolded; they're data. Common readings:

- **Too big** — split it.
- **Wrong time** — life happened; move it.
- **Not actually wanted** — the most useful discovery a check-in can make. Offer to drop it.

Don't pile the missed work onto next week. That's how roadmaps collapse.

## Celebrating what's done

Name completed milestones specifically before moving on. It's easy to skip, and progress
that goes unacknowledged doesn't feel like progress.

## Frequency

Suggest a check-in rhythm based on the plan — usually every 2–4 weeks. Weekly is often too
frequent for 90-day milestones; quarterly lets drift go unnoticed.
